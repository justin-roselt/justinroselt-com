What we do

Six specialties. One platform underneath them.

Digital mailroom & inbound correspondence

eCommunications · oMail

One intake for every channel. Correspondence classified against your rules and put in front of the officer who can act, with the record already filed.

Accounts payable & invoice automation

eFinance on OASIS

Invoices captured from every channel, read, coded, matched to purchase orders, routed for approval and posted clean into your finance system.

Intelligent data extraction

The engine underneath everything

Unstructured documents become structured, queryable data at 99%+ accuracy. No template per supplier to maintain.

Digitisation & scanning programmes

NOVA

Scanning is the easy part. NOVA takes over everything after the scanner: separating, classifying, indexing and registering, with zero manual keying of metadata.

Archiving, records & registration

eArchiver · Files Online

Documents registered natively to Content Manager, TRIM, Objective or SharePoint, with classification and retention metadata applied on the way in.

System integration

eConnect

A data bridge that lets one system talk to another, so automation value reaches the system of record instead of moving the manual burden one step right.
